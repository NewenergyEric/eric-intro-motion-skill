# New Tidekeeper source material — 13 October 2026

The public Art Director project now includes new repair and lantern-raising poses, a night harbor, a separate supply boat, a revised damaged workshop, continuous repair → light → arrival scenes, and an editable campaign title plaque.

[View the published update](https://github.com/NewenergyEric/art-director-skill/blob/04f183050a5d1122648a9c5aa35b998f325bf08f/docs/TIDEKEEPER-PRODUCTION.md) · [English source film](latest-source-en.mp4) · [File provenance](provenance.json)

The 24-second English source film is copied byte-for-byte from that public revision (1280 × 720, 30 fps). The three gallery previews are unretouched frames at 16, 18 and 21.7 seconds. The complete editable source remains in the [upstream repository at the pinned revision](https://github.com/NewenergyEric/art-director-skill/tree/04f183050a5d1122648a9c5aa35b998f325bf08f/motion).

The three native-format comparison films on this site retain their earlier six-scene edit and original provenance. The new source preview is a separate later edit, not evidence that its new scenes have already been recomposed into those three formats.

These are original candidate assets. The upstream project records composition review and technical checks; it does not claim final human art approval or a shipped game. Night scenery has minor generated geometry differences and is presented as a narrative candidate.
